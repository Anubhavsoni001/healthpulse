## 📌 About The Project

MediSense is an AI-powered healthcare platform designed to simplify medical understanding and improve health monitoring for users.

The platform combines Artificial Intelligence, prescription analysis, medical report simplification, and real-time health monitoring into one smart healthcare solution.

It helps users better understand their health data and provides AI-driven healthcare assistance through an easy-to-use interface.

---

#  Features

## 🩻 Prescription Analyzer
- Upload medical prescriptions
- Detect medicines from prescriptions
- Suggest alternative medicines
- Pharmacy assistance support

## 📄 Medical Report Simplifier
- Upload complex medical reports
- Convert reports into easy language
- AI-generated medical summaries
- Ask questions related to reports

## 📊 Real-Time Health Monitoring
- Monitor:
  - SPO2
  - Pulse Rate
  - Body Temperature
- Track historical health data
- AI-based health insights

## 🤖 AI Medical Chatbot
- Healthcare-related conversations
- Basic medical guidance
- User-friendly AI interaction

## 📈 Health Analytics Dashboard
- Visualise health records
- Track health progress
- Compare previous health data
